## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26
### Added
- 13 skills: `creator` router, trends, niche, reels, script, article, humanize, analyze, competitor, hooks, captions, repurpose, calendar.
- 4 tiered agents: creator-researcher (Sonnet), creator-writer (Opus), creator-analyst (Opus), creator-quick (Haiku).
- Keyless `trends.py` (Google Trends, Google News, autocomplete, Reddit, HN, Wikipedia, YouTube RSS).
- `humanize_score.py` and `post_stats.py`, both stdlib-only, with self-tests.
- 14 eval cases for `claude plugin eval`, plus static validation and live-source checks.
