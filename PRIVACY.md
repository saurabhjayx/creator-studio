# Privacy

- **No telemetry.** creator-studio collects nothing and has no server.
- **Outbound requests:** `trends.py` sends your query terms (niche keywords, subreddit names, YouTube channel IDs) to Google, Reddit, Hacker News Algolia, Wikimedia and YouTube, under their own privacy policies. Web searches go through Claude's tools.
- **Your files:** post CSVs, drafts and niche profiles stay on your machine. Skills write only to `./.creator/` and to files you name.
- **Claude:** prompts and file contents you share are processed by Anthropic under your plan's terms.
