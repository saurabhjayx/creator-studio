# Hook formulas (25)

Each: **template** · example · best platform · why it works. Platform hook windows live in
`skills/creator/references/platforms.md`. `[X]` = creator's real number; never invent one.

| # | Name | Template | Example | Best platform | Why it works |
|---|------|----------|---------|---------------|--------------|

## Pairing tips

- Video: say formula A, show text that adds info (not repeats it). Example: spoken #3, on-screen "$0 ads".
- Carousels: slide 1 = hook; slide 2 = second hook (IG re-serves carousels from slide 2).
- LinkedIn/X: the hook is line 1; line 2 must earn the "see more" click.
- Avoid stacking 3+ formulas in one line; clarity in <2s beats cleverness.
