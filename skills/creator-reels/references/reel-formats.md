# 20 proven short-form formats

Platform-agnostic (Reels, Shorts, TikTok). Timings assume a 15-45s cut.
"Hook" = what lands in the first 1.5s, both on-screen text and spoken line.
Specs (aspect, safe zones, caption limits): `skills/creator/references/platforms.md`.

| # | Format | Structure | Best when | Avoid when |
|---|--------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| 1 | **Talking head (direct)** | Hook claim → 2-3 punchy points → payoff → CTA | Opinion, expertise, explainers | Topic needs visual proof |
| 2 | **Green screen explainer** | Creator over screenshot/headline → "here's what this means" → 3 implications | News, trends, studies, tweets | Image rights unclear (use headline text, not photos) |
| 3 | **POV** | On-screen "POV: you ..." → relatable acted scene → twist/punchline | Relatable pain points, humour | Serious/sensitive topics |
| 4 | **Tutorial / how-to** | Show end result first → steps 1-2-3 with on-screen numbers → result again | Skills, recipes, tools, workouts | Steps > 5 (split into a series) |
| 5 | **Before / after** | After shown in 0-1s → "how" → before → process montage → after | Transformations, design, edits, fitness | No real before/after footage |
| 6 | **Listicle (3-5 things)** | "3 X that Y" → fast cuts per item, numbered text → strongest item last | Tools, tips, mistakes, recommendations | Items need >8s each |
| 7 | **Storytime** | Cliffhanger first line → context → tension → resolution → lesson | Personal experience, case studies | Story has no turn |
| 8 | **Duet / stitch / reply** | Clip of other creator/comment (0-3s) → your reaction/correction/addition | Debates, myths, replying to comments | Punching down; rights restrictions |
| 9 | **Myth vs fact** | "Stop believing X" → myth on screen (red) → fact + source (green) | Health, finance, fitness myths | Can't cite the fact |
| 10 | **Mistakes to avoid** | "You're doing X wrong" → mistake demo → fix demo → repeat ×2-3 | Beginners, technique, tools | Audience is advanced |
| 11 | **Day in the life / vlog** | Time-stamped clips → one insight per clip → reflective close | Lifestyle, careers, behind the scenes | No interesting moments |
| 12 | **Reaction** | Trend/news clip or headline → face reaction → opinion in 1 line | Flash (hours) trends | Sensitive news |
| 13 | **Comparison / vs** | Split screen A vs B → 3 criteria → verdict | Products, methods, apps | Sponsored bias not disclosed |
| 14 | **Challenge / trend remix** | Do the trending format/audio with niche twist | Trending audio/format this week | Trend is > 2 weeks old |
| 15 | **Text-on-screen + b-roll (faceless)** | Big text hook → 4-6 text beats over aesthetic b-roll → CTA | Faceless accounts, quotes, tips | Complex explanation |
| 16 | **Screen recording walkthrough** | Result first → cursor-led steps, zoomed → shortcut reveal | Software, AI tools, spreadsheets | Small text unreadable on phone |
| 17 | **Hot take / unpopular opinion** | "Unpopular opinion: X" → 2 reasons → invite debate | Debates, saturated topics | Topic is tragic/partisan |
| 18 | **Q&A / answering a comment** | Comment sticker on screen → direct answer → follow-up question | Audience questions from niche research | No real comment (don't fake one) |
| 19 | **Series episode ("Part N")** | "Part 3 of X" → recap 1 line → new content → tease next | Pillars, multi-step skills | First post of a new account |
| 20 | **Satisfying process / ASMR** | Wide → close-up process → reveal; minimal talking | Cooking, crafts, cleaning, building | Information-heavy topic |

## Hook patterns (pair with any format)

- **Result first**: show the payoff frame at 0s.
- **Negative**: "Stop doing X." / "You're wasting money on X."
- **Specific number**: "I tested 5 X so you don't have to." (only if true)
- **Curiosity gap**: "Nobody talks about this part of X."
- **Callout**: "If you're a <audience> who <pain>, watch this."
- **Contradiction**: "X is actually making your Y worse."

## Strength predictors (for the "predicted strength" field)

Rate High / Medium / Low with a reason drawn from these:
1. Hook clarity: understood with sound off in 1.5s?
2. Trend timing vs shelf life: posting inside the window?
3. Payoff density: new info or visual change every 2-3s?
4. Rewatch/save value: list, recipe, steps, cheat sheet = saves.
5. Comment trigger: debate, question, "which one are you?"
6. Creator fit and production feasibility today (phone only?).

Never predict view numbers.

## Audio guidance

- Talking formats: original audio; optionally low-volume trending track underneath.
- Faceless / process / transition formats: trending audio matters most.
- Audio suggestions come from WebSearch roundups and are always labelled
  "verify trending in-app": trends rotate in days and differ by region; business accounts
  may only have the commercial library.
