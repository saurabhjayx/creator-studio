## What each algorithm rewards (2026, best-effort)

| Platform | Primary signals | Secondary | Penalized |
|----------|-----------------|-----------|-----------|
| IG Reels | Watch time + completion, **sends/shares per reach** (strongest for non-follower reach) | Saves, likes per reach, original audio | Watermarks from other apps, reposted/unoriginal content, engagement bait |
| IG carousel | Swipe-through + saves | Second-chance distribution (shown again from slide 2) | Text-only slide 1 with no hook |
| TikTok | Completion rate, rewatches, watch time | Shares, comments, follows from video, search keyword match (on-screen text + caption + speech) | Off-platform watermarks, slow intros, recycled content |
| YT Shorts | Viewed-vs-swiped-away %, avg % viewed, loops | Subs from short, likes | Reuploaded non-original clips |
| YT long-form | CTR x avg view duration (satisfaction), returning viewers | Session time, comments, search/browse match | Clickbait that tanks retention |
| LinkedIn | Dwell time, meaningful comments (esp. early 60-90 min), expertise relevance | Saves, reposts with thoughts | External links in body (put in first comment), engagement pods, AI-sounding filler |
| X | Replies (esp. author replying back), reposts, bookmarks, dwell | Premium boost, media | External links (reduced reach), hashtag spam |
| Threads | Replies + conversation depth, follows | Topic tag match | Engagement bait |
| Pinterest | Saves, outbound clicks, keyword relevance (title/desc/board) | Fresh pins (new image), seasonality (pin 30-45 days ahead) | Duplicate pins, broken links |
| Blog / SEO | Helpfulness (who/how/why), intent match, E-E-A-T, internal links | Freshness, AI-citation-ready passages | Thin/scaled content, keyword stuffing |

## Hashtag norms

- Mix tiers: **broad** (very large, generic), **mid** (niche-category), **niche** (community-specific/long-tail).
- Volumes are **unknown unless fetched** from a tool; never invent post counts.
- Avoid banned/spammy tags (#followforfollow, #like4like, #f4f, #l4l, #instagood-style mega-tags add nothing), and
  anything flagged by the platform search ("recent posts hidden").
- Hashtags on TikTok/IG now act mostly as **search keywords**; spoken words + on-screen text carry the same weight.

## Safe zones (vertical video)

Keep text/faces inside the center ~1080x1420 of a 1080x1920 frame: top ~220px (UI) and bottom ~280-400px
(caption + buttons) get covered. Right edge ~120px holds action buttons on TikTok/Reels.

## Cross-posting rules

- Remove watermarks before cross-posting (IG, YT both downrank them).
- Rewrite captions natively per platform; the same text everywhere reads as spam.
- Stagger posts by a few hours to a day when cross-posting the same video.
